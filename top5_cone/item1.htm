#1 Hollow Knight: Silksong

Hollow Knight: Silksong
Hollow Knight: Silksong is a masterpiece of a game by Team Charry and the successor to Hollow Knight. If you're looking for a metroidvania game, this is a great game for that, though with the difficulty curve, it might scare off some but once you get past that, the story behind it and it's fantastic world is beautiful.
  • Simple yet beautiful 2D graphics
  • Elegant combat mechanics
  • A beautiful story that fills the world with wonder

#4 Celeste

Celeste
Celeste is a platformer by Extremely OK Games that compacts difficulty and challenges in single screen to screen levels. It also has beautiful story about learning to accept youself. If you are looking for a difficult platformer, then this is what I would suggest.
  • Pixelated 2D game
  • Challenging platformer
  • An amazing story

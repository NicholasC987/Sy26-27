#5 Undertale

Undertale
Undertale is a role-playing game by Toby Fox that gives you choices that can completely change your ending. The game can also remember what you did in past runs, making it quite the interesting experience. With the choice to kill or show mercy, you make your own story through its wonderful world.
  • Pixelated 2D game
  • Build your own story
  • Interesting story about choice
